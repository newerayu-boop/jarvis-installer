How to stop being lazy 🥱

(1) The one question that you need to ask yourself when you're sitting down to do something and suddenly you feel this laziness coming in and you feel like you don't want to do that is:"Do I really have to do that?" My laziness kicks in when I start doing things that can be delegated or things that are useless because when I'm super excited about something (e.g. vlogging, running and growing my businesses, trying out new things), you can't stop me. You need to ask yourself:"Do I like what I'm doing?" Laziness is just protecting you from doing unnecessary things or things that could be done better by somebody else.

(2)  You really need those lazy days. There are some influencers who are promoting the "no weekend" lifestyle which I find unhealthy. On Saturdays, I can't work the full mode because I see relaxed people and my my body enters this "lazy" mode. I don't punish myself because it is the worst thing you can do to yourself. Relaxing doesn't mean being lazy. You have to relax, you have to give yourself some rest because our brain enters this "meditation" mode and it starts to relax. This what helps find new ways to improve your life.

(3) Another great thing that would help you stop being lazy is to constantly explore different worlds. For example, in 2014, I discovered YouTube and I realized this is a completely new world, I started thinking of what I can do on this social media platform. Every year, I take up something new. You never know what is going to help you overcome laziness and become more motivated and productive.

(4) Your environment gives you additional energy, ideas, kick in the butt to start working because you get inspired by other people. I just wished you guys figure out what is this place in the world for you, where do you feel most productive.

(5) Another great lifehack if you feel lazy is to have a cup of coffee.
