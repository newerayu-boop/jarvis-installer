# Структура видео и интервью

Синтез по 100 видео канала Silicon Valley Girl (Марина Могилко). Все цитаты — дословные, на английском. Документ описывает два главных каркаса: соло-видео (листикл/эссе) и интервью/подкаст, плюс словарь переходов, финалы и чек-листы.

---

## 1. Каноническая анатомия соло-видео (листикл)

Доминирующий формат канала — "TOP N / N ideas / N skills / N jobs". Полный скелет:

### 1.1. Хук (0:00–0:30)
Цифра-шок / личная история / страх из отчёта (см. документ 01-hooks.md).

### 1.2. Гейт интереса
"so if you're interested continue watching this video" / "please watch this video up to the very end". Иногда + ранний CTA: "before we go into the topic please don't forget to hit the like button for the youtube algorithm" (15 Most In-Demand Jobs in 2021, 464 000).

### 1.3. Право говорить (авторитет)
Личная цифра или инсайдерская позиция: "i live in silicon valley and i talk to entrepreneurs on a daily basis and i keep asking them what are the problems on the market that they see" (Top 10 profitable business ideas for 2024, 128 000).

### 1.4. Анонс каркаса + принцип ранжирования + двойной open loop
Структура всегда проговаривается вслух, включая порядок сортировки:
- "I basically arranged all of those ideas going from the hardest ones to start... going to the easiest ones" (10 profitable business ideas 2024, 175 000).
- "we're going to start with job number 17 and go all the way to job number one and at the end of the video i'm also going to talk about jobs that have no future" (17 Most in-demand jobs, 639 000).
- "I will be ranking these income streams from the ones that make me the least right now to the ones that make me the most" (9 Passive Income Ideas, 591 000).
- "top 17 highest paying jobs of the future we rank them by median salary" (TOP 17 Highest Paying Jobs, 806 000).

**Ранжирование по возрастанию — встроенный механизм удержания**: №1 (самая большая зарплата / любимая идея / крупнейший доход) зритель узнаёт только в конце. Плюс анонс бонуса в финале ("jobs that have no future") — второй open loop. Плюс open loop на последний пункт: "And trust me, the last one is a total game changer. So, let's dive in." (9 AI Skills, 142 000).

В поздних видео анонс каркаса становится явным roadmap: "So, in this video, I pulled the exact playbook from people who've actually done it. What to build, how to find your niche, how to get customers with zero budget, and when you actually need investors. Spoiler, probably never." (Start a $10K/Month AI Business, 126 000). И: "I'll break down six AI business models from the fastest ones you can start this week... For each, I'll talk about what it is, why it works now, and how you'd actually get your first client" (6 Profitable AI business ideas for 2026, 77 000).

### 1.5. Нумерованные пункты (тело)
Каждый пункт открывается словесным маркером ("Business idea number one...") и строится по микроструктуре (см. раздел 3). Между пунктами — мини-уроки бизнес-мышления и мини-загадки: "the next business idea hits 74,000 Google searches last month do you know what it is" (TOP 9 small business ideas, 1 400 000).

### 1.6. Спонсор в середине (после 1/3–1/2 видео)
Никогда «в лоб» — всегда через мостик от только что обсуждённой боли:
- Вход: "our next business ideas is at game changers but first let's thank the sponsor of this video" (10 business ideas 2024, 175 000); "Let's pause for a quick tip." (5 BEST AI Businesses, 311 000); "quick ad break here" (TOP 6 Digital Product Ideas, 95 000).
- Внутри: личный кейс использования + цифры + промокод с именем ("use my code marina3 to save 30%", "use code SVG30 to get 30% off").
- Выход: "and now let's get back to our business ideas we have so many more and some of my absolute favorites" (175 000); "Now, let's move on to the next AI skill you absolutely need in 2025." (142 000).
- Спонсор может маскироваться под очередной пункт списка: "The next tool I wanted to talk about is called Popto. And Popto is sponsoring this video." (I Spent $10K Testing 100+ AI Tools, 152 000) или под «бизнес-идею №2»: "this is a huge market opportunity and there is actually a company doing this and the next segment of this video is sponsored by them the company is called koinly" (Top 10 profitable business ideas, 128 000).
- Если спонсора нет — его место занимает свой продукт: курс ("if you want to start I have a course the link is down below"), лид-магнит email ("I have an email list where we'll share more about business ideas"), newsletter Futureproof, консультация, аффилиатные ссылки.

### 1.7. Мид-ролл лайк-аск / engagement перед топ-2
"we're coming to top two business ideas they're actually my favorites so uh like this video if you're still watching" (175 000). Или мини-опрос: "quick comment down below let's do this mini research can you comment down below what type of family you come from" (12 saving tips, 99 000).

### 1.8. Бонус-блок (обещанный в хуке)
"now let's talk about jobs that are fading away" — выплата второго open loop (TOP 17, 806 000).

### 1.9. Финал-задание
Резюме/манифест → задание в комментарии → подписка → мост на следующее видео (подробно — раздел 5).

## 2. Полная структура интервью/подкаста

Формат 2024–2026, выкристаллизовавшийся из ранних влог-интервью.

### 2.1. Тизер-нарезка (cold open)
3–6 самых острых цитат гостя до заставки, часто с оборванным ответом: "But to be in the top 0.1 percent, you will have to —" (Stanford AI Expert, 133 000). Может заканчиваться интригой: "So, the real question is, what happens to us next?" (CEO of Microsoft AI, 239 000).

### 2.2. Закадровое представление гостя (досье)
Формула «This is [имя] + регалии + цифры + тезис-дедлайн»: "This is Aaron Levy, founder of Box... 64% of the Fortune 500 uses his platform. He says we have 3 years to build the next generation of AI companies." (117 000). Драматургия контраста: "this guy failed most of his classes at high school... and now he collected all of the badges in Silicon Valley that you can think of" (1 800 000).

### 2.3. Однострочный дисклеймер спонсора
"This video is sponsored by HubSpot." — сразу после тизера, до разговора ($215M AI CEO, 510 000; CEO of Microsoft AI, 239 000).

### 2.4. Приветствие + самопрезентация гостя
- "Mustafa, welcome. Thank you so much for being here." (239 000).
- Ритуальный запрос: **"Can you introduce yourself in 60 seconds?"** ($215M AI CEO, 510 000).
- Либо айсбрейкер/личная связь: "When I Googled your name, you know what Google said? TV actor." (Tim Draper, 107 000); "Samir, welcome to the podcast... How old are you now? I'm 24. 24. I'm getting old. My knees start to hurt." (AI Instead of a Degree, 508 000).
- Иногда + сбор аудитории: "Let me know in the comments down below where you watching from and what is your biggest goal right now?" (508 000).

### 2.5. Первый вопрос — самый горячий, от боли аудитории
- "The first question, everyone's talking about it on YouTube right now. Are we in an AI bubble?" (239 000).
- "a lot of people wake up in the middle of the night worried that AI is taking away their job... what should people be doing to stop this anxiety?" (Mel Robbins, 106 000).
- "do you think it's our last chance to build wealth right now?" (Gary Vee, 205 000).
- Рамочный вопрос о пользе для зрителя: "can you tell me if we do something today, how is somebody's life different in a month once they deployed everything we're going to talk about?" (Ex-Amazon AI Leader, 234 000).
- Вход через свежий пост/арку гостя: "5 days ago, you posted this. We're at a unique moment of history..." (Aaron Levie, 117 000); "Can you start from the beginning because you're from India? Uh can you talk about your childhood?" (Sampriti, 630 000).

### 2.6. Дуга беседы: от макро к личному к практике
Типовой порядок: большой тезис/тренд → страхи и возражения → биография гостя (иммигрантская арка) → механика бизнеса и цифры → практический плейбук для зрителя → семья/личное → блиц. Марина постоянно меняет масштаб: "судьба человечества → её кухня → её дети → её холестерин" — "Imagine it's 2040 and we're in this kitchen. What things are obsolete?"; "Should I be saving for my kids college or not? They're five and four." (239 000).

### 2.7. Марина как прокси зрителя и адвокат дьявола
Роли, которые она играет вопросами:
- **Прокси-новичок**: "That was a very sophisticated prompt. How do I know that I needed that prompt? Cuz I would never think about API keys." (Head of ChatGPT & Codex, 134 000); "I don't have any experience in quantum physics, but the way I understood it, if you throw a ball against the wall, it bumps, right?" (Nobel Prize Winner, 154 000); "Can anyone build this, or does it require technical knowledge?" (234 000).
- **Адвокат дьявола**: "But how like when I think about this, like why would I start if it's already being automated?" (Gary Vee, 205 000); "but we kind of had the same thought you know during crash like we're inventing internet blah blah blah but there are some companies that you know their valuations are much higher than their revenues" (239 000); "I talked to godfather of AI... He's like, 'We have two years.' Like, what are they not getting?" (117 000).
- **Неудобные вопросы про деньги**: "what is the minimum amount of money for you per month you need to feel comfortable" (Alex Mehr, 88 000); "you mentioned that you're making from 250 to 350 a month yeah on youtube can you break this" (Graham Stephan, 155 000); "how close are you to that goal if you can share" (YourRichBFF, 546 000).
- **Провокация чужой критикой**: "I also read Amazon reviews and some people are like this guy can not really teach us entrepreneurship because he came to this world of entrepreneurship being backed by his family" (Tim Draper, 107 000); "What about the controversy about your brand deals? I just watched like coffee SE all the what was your reaction to that" (Jake Tran, 222 000).
- **Вопросы через собственные страхи**: "basically my self-worth is really dependent on my productivity and how much money I'm making" (Mel Robbins, 106 000); "I want to ask my questions as a mom, as an immigrant" (Future of Work 2026, 295 000).
- **Вопросы от команды/аудитории**: "I have this question from my team who read your piece AI splits people into architects and masons... And half of my team thinks they're masons" (205 000); "i had a question from a follower" (155 000).
- **Перефраз-суммирование**: "so basically you start with expertise... you need to be deep in that market" (510 000); "So basically a chief of staff" (234 000).
- **Просьба определить термины**: "Let's define skill, by the way, cuz we mentioned it several times" (234 000); "can you describe Transformers within search like what what do you mean by that" (881 000).
- **Немедленное присвоение советов**: "Okay I'm going to call my COO after this interview and I'm going to change process I love it" (508 000); "I'm going to do this right after the interview." (133 000).

### 2.8. Просьбы о демо
Живая демонстрация в кадре — фирменный блок: "Can you show me a workflow that everyone should be deploying today?... Can can you build something for me something like that?" (134 000); "Let's actually build something" (234 000); Марина грузит собственный контент в продукт гостя: "probably takes about like 30 to 60 minutes >> oh 30 to 60 minutes... I'm going to show you the result." (510 000). Плюс личные просьбы: "can you look at our setup and tell me how sophisticated it is and which level it is?" (234 000).

### 2.9. Закадровые пояснения посреди беседы
Уникальный приём: Марина монтирует внутрь интервью объяснительные вставки-эссе для широкой аудитории: "So, not only it's electric and it kind of flies about the water... she's building the whole ecosystem." (Sampriti, 630 000); "longtime subscribers note a decrease in the quality of content compared to old videos" (Jake Tran, 222 000); "That's the moment quantum stopped being just theory. He proved it works in machines and that's what started everything." (154 000).

### 2.10. Спонсор в середине + мид-ролл CTA
Мостик из только что сказанного: "Let me pause here for a second because what we've been talking about connects directly to something my team has been actively testing." (205 000); "Allie just walked us through her setup. 36 proactive workflows around 100 agents are running while she sleeps. And here's the thing every team hits when they try to build something like that." (234 000). Возврат всегда формульный: "Now, back to our conversation with Tibo." / "Now, let's get back to Aaron." Плюс мид-ролл подписка с позиционированием: "If you want to stay ahead in the AI era, I bring you the people who see what's coming next every week. Subscribe to this channel so you don't miss it." (205 000) и лид-магнит: "if you want to just copy and paste whatever you should be doing with your Claude, subscribe to our newsletter and we're going to send you the most actionable email from this episode. The link is down below." (234 000).

### 2.11. Ритуальные закрывающие вопросы
Фиксированный набор (2–4 на эпизод):
- **Совет себе в 20 / принцип**: "what is one principle that you wish you understood when you were 20 that would saved you years?" (510 000).
- **Книга/фильм**: "can you recommend one book or movie that everyone absolutely has to see or read?" (630 000).
- **Блиц**: "I have a few questions that are fire starters... Rapid" — "What are your top three favorite AI tools that you use daily?", "College degrees in 2026, yes or no?", "One industry to stay away from?" (205 000).
- **Дети/колледж**: "You have a six-year-old, right?... Are they going to go to college?" (117 000).
- **Совет для стартующих**: "Okay. Last question, advice for entrepreneurs who are starting today." (117 000); "what would be your advice to someone sitting and watching in India and willing to come to the US to build a startup" (117 000, Pesto).
- **Детская мечта / личное**: "do you think you're living your childhood dream?", "your favorite podcast or YouTube channel?" (1 800 000).
- **Финальный «один совет»**: "So, let's wrap up this interview with one advice for everyone... What is the one skill that they should be learning?" (508 000).

### 2.12. Мораль от Марины одним предложением
"the one thing that you should learn from this interview is that you have to love what you do that's i think that's the most important" (155 000); "The most important thing is trusting myself as well." (106 000); "This is something I'll be teaching my kids." (510 000).

### 2.13. Финал-переадресация на другой эпизод

- "And if this gave you the plan, the next episode gives you the speed. Alex Mashrabov breaks down exactly how to go from zero to 1 million ARR... Oh, watch it right here." (126 000).
Плюс переток в экосистему: LinkedIn гостя ("I will leave your LinkedIn I know when kha came to my podcast his LinkedIn was flooded by DMS"), newsletter с выжимками ("I actually have a newsletter that goes out every time we have a new video where I share most actionable takeaways from every interview", 508 000).

## 3. Микроструктура пункта соло-видео

Каждый нумерованный пункт собирается из четырёх кирпичей:

1. **Тезис + маркер**: "Business idea number two...", "Skill number one...", "rule number ten".
2. **Личная или звёздная история**: "did you know how we got our first client I was 21 years old back in the University..." (How to start a business in 2 days, 97 000); "every Friday she would go to her agencies and just give them those cookies... the agencies were like, 'Oh, of course we have Carly. Let's give her that contract.'" (Life-Changing Lessons, 90 000). Формула: «совет + вот как я на этом обожглась/выиграла».
3. **Цифра**: размер рынка, зарплата, ставка, юнит-экономика — "this Market is already a $55 billion Market... set to increase to 456 billion by 2031" (175 000); "salary for this position is 78840 dollars and there are 6560 job openings right now" (113 000); "people are ready to pay up to 48 dollars an hour for sales funnels manager" (622 000).
4. **Action step**: "And here's your action step" — микрозадание: "Record 10 minutes of your voice, clone it with 11 Labs, and list it on Fiverr or voices.com." (5 BEST AI Businesses, 311 000); "After you watch this video, spend at least 10 minutes VIP coding" (142 000). Иногда — готовый скрипт продаж: "I counted you missed that many calls this lunch. I can fix that for $500 a month" (77 000).

Поздняя эксплицитная формула пункта: **"what it is, why it works now, and how you'd actually get your first client"** (77 000). Шаблон пункта в джобс-листиклах: «что делает специалист (бытовой пример) → зарплата → % роста спроса → порог входа/как войти».

В компиляциях из подкастов пункт = «мостик-вопрос → клип гостя → авторская выжимка»: "Clear pattern shows up. AI is not killing entire professions. It's killing the shallow version of those professions." (295 000).

## 4. Маркеры переходов (дословный словарь)

### Нумерация и движение по списку

- "the next business idea is..." / "the next one is my favorite" / "my next personal favorite is of course of course of course perplexity"
- "and the last but not the least..." (фирменный маркер последнего пункта)
- "are you ready for number one" / "we're approaching some of the highest paid jobs"
- "Let's move up in complexity" / "now let's move on to the actual ideas"
- "Okay, tool number 11" / "let's start with number one"

### Вход в спонсорский блок
- "but first let's thank the sponsor of this video"
- "Let me pause here for a second because what we've been talking about connects directly to..."
- "quick ad break here" / "quick ad right here this video is brought to you by..."
- "Let's pause for a quick tip."
- "guys I want to quickly pause here and thank one of my favorite companies in the world..."
- "Speaking of separating roles..." / "and speaking of taking action now..."
- "Before we continue, let's talk about something critical for any business..."
- "I want to pause here for a second because while I was preparing for this conversation with Mel, something clicked for me."

### Выход из спонсорского блока
- "and now let's get back to our business ideas"
- "Now, back to our conversation with Tibo." / "and now let's get back to our conversation with aush"
- "let's get back to the video" / "now let's get back to the video"
- "All right, domain sorted. Now, let's continue building our business."
- "And now, back to my conversation with Kian."

### Смысловые мостики между блоками

### Внутри интервью

- "I'm glad that you mentioned it cuz I was thinking a lot..."
- "let's wrap up this conversation by talking about your team" (анонс финального блока)

### Open loop-маркеры
- "trust me, the last one is a total game changer"
- "make sure you watch until the end" / "please watch this video up to the very end"
- "at the end of this video, I'll share my own plan"
- "this next insight is going to change everything for you"
- "I'm going to mention some of those things closer to the end of this video"
- "keep watching" / "so keep watching"

## 5. Финалы

Стандартный финал соло-видео складывается из 4–6 элементов (порядок стабилен):

### 5.1. Благодарность за досмотр (вознаграждение выжившим)
- "thank you so much for watching this video up to the very end" — фирменная формула, присутствует почти в каждом видео.
- Вариант-опечатка поздних видео: "thank you for watching this after the very end. Bye." (154 000).

### 5.2. Задание в комментарии (а не просто «подпишись»)
- "please let me know down in the comments below what was your favorite business idea" (175 000).
- "comment down below with your business idea and why you think it's impossible to start it without any cash and I would get back to you with my solution to your problem" (102 000).
- "I will pick up three weirdest things that you guys come up with in comments below and Tell you how you can make business out of it" (225 000).
- "If you want me to break down specific AI workflows for your job or industry, tell me what you do in the comments. I might make a whole video around it." (295 000).
- "drop a comment below sharing what you're going to focus on... and we're going to comment with different AI tools that could help with that" (191 000).
- Конкурсы: "write down the comment below with your idea... I'm gonna pick an idea and I'm gonna promote it on my Instagram I currently have over three hundred thirty thousand followers" (445 000); "I decided to give you $100 to start your company" (107 000).

### 5.3. Подписка/лайк — фирменные формулы
- "hit the red button down below" / "there is a red button down below hit it and subscribe" / "the red button is below it's a big red button"
- "smash the like button" / "please smash the red subscribe button down below to not miss my next vlogs"
- "if you're not yet subscribed to this channel hit subscribe"
- Подписка через ценность (поздние видео): "Subscribe to this channel because we're releasing tons of interesting podcasts with people who are actually moving AI and building these tools for you. You don't want to miss them." (152 000).

### 5.4. Челлендж / вдохновляющий манифест
- "my challenge for you is start a business in today's like go ahead do it" (97 000).
- "Pick one idea, launch your MVP this week, and test it with real people... The AI gold rush isn't coming. It is here. And the only question is will you watch it happen or will you be part of it?... So off we go. Let's build our wealth." (311 000).
- "Choose one principle from this video and commit to implementing it over the next 90 days." (191 000).
- "Your career is not over. It's just in beta and you're the one shipping the next version." (295 000).
- "being human is your superpower. How cool is that? Doesn't it feel great to hear that?" (230 000).
- "don't be afraid to start in 2026 cuz this is definitely the best time to build" (96 000).

### 5.5. Мост на следующий контент / экосистему
- "meanwhile watch how much i made last year on youtube the link is below see you soon bye-bye" (80 000).
- "watch my interview with Alex Muir he launches businesses every week... the link will be below" (97 000).
- "if you haven't seen my previous video it's gonna be here and if you want to see a video about three questions... it's gonna be here some click watch" (389 000).
- Newsletter: "do not forget to subscribe to my email newsletter the link will be down below I share all the things that are working for me and my team" (96 000); "I put together a free 30-day implementation plan... it's waiting for you in my newsletter, completely free. The newsletter is called Futureproof." (119 000).
- Instagram: "please follow me on Instagram lingual Marina" / "don't forget to follow me on instagram lingua marina".

### 5.6. Прощание
"and I will see you very soon in my next videos bye" / "see you soon in the next vlogs bye" / "bye-bye" / "I'll see you very soon on this channel bye".

**Финал интервью** отличается: мораль-вывод от Марины → благодарность гостю ("Thank you so much. It was amazing.") → соцсети гостя → end-screen на смежный эпизод + комментарии как метрика перетока ("let me know in the comments if you came from this episode with Gary").

## 6. Чек-лист структуры: соло-видео

1. [ ] Хук с конкретной цифрой в первых 20 секундах (сумма/дата/процент).
2. [ ] Гейт интереса или двойной open loop ("watch up to the very end because...").
3. [ ] Право говорить: личная цифра/инсайдерская позиция ("I live in Silicon Valley...", "I've been running my own business since 2011").
4. [ ] Анонс каркаса вслух + принцип ранжирования (по возрастанию: лучшее — в конце).
5. [ ] Анонс бонуса в финале (второй open loop: "at the end of the video I'm also going to talk about...").
6. [ ] Каждый пункт по микроструктуре: маркер-нумерация → тезис → личная/звёздная история → цифра → action step.
7. [ ] Мини-загадки и open loops между пунктами ("do you know what it is").
8. [ ] Спонсор после 1/3–1/2, вход через мостик от боли, личное демо, промокод, формульный выход ("now let's get back to...").
9. [ ] Если спонсора нет — свой продукт/лид-магнит/аффилиаты на его месте.
10. [ ] Мид-ролл лайк-аск или вопрос в комменты перед топ-2 ("like this video if you're still watching").
11. [ ] 1–2 правила-теглайна на видео ("over deliver every time", "AI becomes your co-founder").
12. [ ] Диалог с воображаемым зрителем и снятие возражений ("you might be wondering, so Marina...").
13. [ ] Выплата всех обещанных open loops (бонус-блок).
14. [ ] Финал: благодарность "up to the very end" → задание в комментарии → подписка ("red button") → челлендж/манифест → мост на следующее видео/newsletter → "bye".

## 7. Чек-лист структуры: интервью

1. [ ] Cold open: 3–6 острейших цитат гостя, желательно с деньгами; одна — оборвана на полуслове.
2. [ ] Закадровое досье: "This is [имя]" + регалии + цифры ("64% of the Fortune 500 uses his platform") + тезис-дедлайн.
3. [ ] Однострочный дисклеймер спонсора ("This video is sponsored by X.").
4. [ ] Личная ставка Марины: почему она, почему сейчас ("I've been preparing for this interview for 8 years").
5. [ ] Приветствие + "Can you introduce yourself in 60 seconds?" (или айсбрейкер из домашней работы).
6. [ ] Первый вопрос — самый горячий, от боли аудитории ("Are we in an AI bubble?").
7. [ ] Дуга: макро-тезис → возражения → биография (иммигрантская арка) → цифры бизнеса → практический плейбук → личное/семья.
8. [ ] Роль прокси-зрителя: «глупые» вопросы, признание некомпетентности, просьбы о бенчмарках и правилах.
9. [ ] Роль адвоката дьявола: пушбек, чужая критика, неудобные вопросы про деньги.
10. [ ] Просьба о демо в кадре ("Can you build something for me?") — на своём контенте/бизнесе.
11. [ ] Закадровые пояснительные вставки для широкой аудитории посреди беседы.
12. [ ] Спонсор в середине через мостик из разговора + "Now, back to our conversation with [имя]". Мид-ролл подписка + newsletter-лид-магнит.
13. [ ] Немедленное присвоение советов ("I'm going to call my COO after this interview").
14. [ ] Ритуальные закрывающие вопросы: совет себе в 20 / книга-фильм / блиц / дети-колледж / "Last question, advice for entrepreneurs who are starting today".
15. [ ] Мораль от Марины одним предложением.
16. [ ] Финал-переадресация: end-screen на смежный эпизод + "let me know in the comments if you came from this episode" + соцсети гостя + newsletter с выжимками.
