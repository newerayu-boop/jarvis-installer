🧠 Science has shown that at most we can hold 2-4 tasks in our minds at any given time. In this video, I share the Todoist, Notion, and Google Calendar workflow I follow everyday that helps me remember everything I need to do and most importantly, get things done! 

So how do I never forget a single task? Simple. CPR. 

1️⃣ Capture: Write down thoughts and ideas the instant they formulate in your mind (I use Todoist, but any to-do app will do) 

2️⃣ Process: Place those thoughts and ideas in their corresponding buckets 

3️⃣ Review: Figure out which ones to act on, which ones to archive

Since reading David Allen's book Getting Things Done, I have adopted a version of his Getting Things Done (GTD) system that works wonders for me. While I am nowhere close to perfect, I have noticed the only tasks I do forget now are the ones I do not capture down immediately

So this method definitely works if you are curious on how to be more productive, how to concentrate more (at school or at work). Or you might simply wanted to learn how to use Todoist and Notion, and curious about the Todoist and Notion workflows I use in my GTD system!
