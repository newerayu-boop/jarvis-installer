# Монетизация и CTA

Как Jeff Su зарабатывает на канале, не теряя доверия: спонсоры — только в середине и только через смысловой мостик; собственные продукты — лестницей от бесплатного шаблона до платного курса; каждый CTA обёрнут в пользу или шутку. Ниже — полная механика с дословными примерами из 100 разобранных видео.

---

## 1. Правило середины: реклама на ~40–60% хронометража, никогда в начале

У Джеффа нет прероллов. Первые минуты видео — всегда чистая ценность (хук → план → 1–2 полноценных пункта). Спонсор или self-promo появляется только после того, как зритель уже что-то получил, — как правило, между пунктами в середине списка:

- **«Notion was HARD until I Learned These 8 Tips!»** — спонсор Notion AI встроен как совет №4 из 8, то есть ровно в середину листикла.
- **«Here's the BIGGEST Mistake Found on Resumes!»** — Teal вставлен между первым кейсом и вторым примером, с явным возвратом к контенту: *"back to coming up with relevant metrics."*
- **«Job Seekers on LinkedIn Need to Know These 8 Things»** — Dex стоит между фичами №4 и №5.
- **«99% of Beginners Don't Know the Basics of AI»** — Coursera между takeaway №1 и №2.
- **«This Simple File Management System Changed My Life!»** — Oakywood между фреймворком и пятью советами, с возвратом: *"but for now back to the digital world."*
- **«How I'd Learn AI From Scratch in 2026»** — HubSpot после Level 1 из трёх уровней.
- **«10 Mac Apps You Haven't Heard Of»** — Dropbox фактически «пунктом №3» списка, с выходом *"...thank you Dropbox again for sponsoring this portion of the video. moving on."*

Единственное упоминание спонсора в начале — короткая честная пометка в хуке, без самой рекламы: *"Let's get started and thank you HubSpot for sponsoring a portion of this video"* («3 AI Habits») — интеграция всё равно идёт в середине.

Ключевые признаки вставки:
1. Вставка идёт **после** выдачи ценности (обычно после 1–2 пунктов или целого уровня/шага).
2. Вход — через смысловой мостик (см. раздел 2), а не через разрыв.
3. Выход — благодарность (*"Thank you X for sponsoring this video / this portion of the video"*) и явный возврат к теме (*"back to...", "moving on"*).
4. Реклама сама по себе полезна («реклама как контент»): бесплатный ebook, cheat sheet, промокоды, скидка 40%.

## 2. Механика нативного мостика: «Speaking of X... today's sponsor...»

Спонсор никогда не появляется «в лоб» — предыдущий пункт контента становится трамплином. Фирменные связки: **"Speaking of X..."**, **"By the way..."**, **"This connects to something interesting..."**, **"...and that's where today's sponsor comes in"**. Все дословные переходы:

### HubSpot
- «4 ChatGPT Hacks»: *"Speaking of writing better prompts, today's sponsor, HubSpot, has an awesome resource I'm pretty impressed with. It's their free ebook for professionals..."* → выход: *"I'll leave a link to the free ebook in the description, and thank you HubSpot for sponsoring this portion of the video."*
- «The Only AI Tools You Need»: *"Speaking of matching the right tool to the task, today's sponsor HubSpot put together a free guide called the AI productivity stack that covers 50 tools organized by use case. Here's why I like it..."* → *"Thank you, HubSpot, for sponsoring this video."*
- «NotebookLM Changed Completely» — мостик через ранее сказанное в самом видео: *"earlier I mentioned that if you want to use deep research, you're better off doing it in Gemini directly. Today's sponsor, HubSpot, actually put together a free guide that maps out exactly how that works... The guide is completely free, so I'll leave a link down below. Thank you, HubSpot, for sponsoring this video."*
- «3 AI Habits»: *"If you're enjoying these strategies, I recommend checking out the AI Agents Unleash Playbook from HubSpot... I actually took a lot of inspiration from this resource for my last two AI agents videos. So, it's kind of awesome they agreed to be a sponsor."*
- «95% of People STILL Prompt ChatGPT-5 Wrong»: *"Quick side note, if you're serious about mastering these techniques, then I highly recommend checking out HubSpot's free playbook on prompt engineering... Thank you HubSpot again for sponsoring a portion of this video."*
- «How I'd Learn AI From Scratch in 2026» — спонсорство обёрнуто в совместный лид-магнит: *"By the way, I'm kind of proud of this. I actually partnered with today's sponsor, HubSpot, in putting together a Google Gemini cheat sheet for all of you... The cheat sheet is completely free, link in the description. Thank you, HubSpot, for sponsoring this video."*

### Notion AI
- «Notion was HARD until I Learned These 8 Tips!»: *"I've been extremely impressed with the new notion Ai and I'm super lucky they agreed to sponsor this video because I probably would have talked about them anyways"* → CTA: *"if you want to try this out for yourself click the link below to unlock the new notion AI in your workspace."*

### Teal
- «Here's the BIGGEST Mistake Found on Resumes!» — мостик от только что показанного шаблона: *"if you want to use that exact structure I highly recommend using the Resume Builder tool from today's sponsor Teal... I actually convinced teal to make changes to their Resume Builder to fit these standards... anyways sign up using the link down below give them a try let me know what you think."*
- «Top 5 LinkedIn Profile Tips! (backed by data)» — спонсор внутри совета №3 как Pro tip: *"pick the profile you want to emulate the most copy the URL create a free account on Teal the only job search company I've agreed to a sponsorship with"* + *"I've talked about teal many times already I love their product... teal is completely free to use check them out using the link down below."*

### Coursera
- «My Simple Claude Cowork System» — мостик через главный концепт видео: *"everything we built today revolves around one important concept, relevant context. And that's something I learned firsthand from today's sponsor, Coursera."* → *"it's taught by actual Google instructors and not random influencers. Not that there's anything wrong with that. Click the link below to get 40% off Coursera Plus. And thank you Coursera for sponsoring this video."*
- «Top 5 ChatGPT Use Cases» — мостик через боль: *"but even with AI assistance managing complex projects and information overload can be a real challenge in the workplace and that's where today's sponsor corsera comes in... check out the link down below and thank you Cera for sponsoring this portion of the video."*
- «Master Data Analysis with ChatGPT» — спонсор заведён в начале как источник знаний (*"after taking the top AI for data analysis course on Corsera"*) и закрыт в конце оффером: *"sign up for Corsera using the link in the description to take advantage of my special offer of 40% off for 3 months of Corsera Plus."*
- «My Top 5 AI Tips for iPhone Users!»: *"Speaking of being productive, a lot of you ask how I stay current in the world of AI... And that's where today's sponsor, Corsera, comes in."*
- «Master Gemini 3.1 for Work» — мостик через статистику: *"This connects to something interesting. According to LinkedIn, people management is now the number one skill employers are looking for in the age of AI. And roles requiring these skills typically pay $32,000 more per year... Huge thanks to Corsera for sponsoring this portion of the video."*
- «The CORRECT way to use ChatGPT (in 2026)» — мостик через вопрос аудитории: *"But a lot of you do ask, 'Do you have an AI course I can take?' And although I don't, this video sponsor, Corsera, does."* → *"Click the link in the description to get started. Thank you, Corsera, for sponsoring this video."*
- «99% of Beginners Don't Know the Basics of AI» — спонсор как продолжение инсайдерского лайфхака («я заплатил, чтобы вы не платили»): *"...who is kindly sponsoring this portion of the video... click the link below to enroll in the Google project management certification and unlock the AI Essentials course for free thank you corsera for sponsoring this part of the video."*

### Scribe
- «Top 20 MacBook Tips» — мостик от предыдущего совета: *"Speaking of recording your screen, the sponsor of today's video is Scribe. I've talked about them before, big fan of their product."* + бонус аудитории: *"they've been super nice and provided us with 200 Scribe Pro promo codes."*

### Прочие (та же механика)
- Dex («Job Seekers on LinkedIn...») — вход через живую историю с другом: *"by the way check this out this is show he's pretty big on take talk and I know I caught up with him two months ago because of this LinkedIn companion app called Dex... I'm super lucky Dex agreed to sponsor this video because I've been looking for a tool like this."*
- Dropbox («10 Mac Apps You Haven't Heard Of»): *"speaking of simple and secure file Management Solutions I've been using Dropbox since I started YouTube years ago so I'm pretty excited they're sponsoring this portion of the video."*
- Oakywood («This Simple File Management System...») — мостик «цифровое ↔ физическое»: *"as we decode our digital space I also want to talk about the products I've been using from Oki wood to declutter my physical space. they are sponsoring this video but I would have talked about them anyways."*

**Анатомия мостика:** тема пункта → связка ("Speaking of X / By the way / and that's where...") → представление спонсора с личной историей использования → чем ресурс полезен (часто с демо) → CTA "link down below / in the description" → благодарность → возврат ("back to... / moving on").

## 3. Формулы доверия

Джефф систематически «прививает» аудиторию от недоверия к рекламе. Повторяющиеся формулы:

**«Я бы всё равно об этом рассказал»:**
- *"I'm super lucky they agreed to sponsor this video because I probably would have talked about them anyways"* (Notion AI)
- *"they are sponsoring this video but I would have talked about them anyways"* (Oakywood)
- *"I've talked about them before, big fan of their product"* (Scribe)
- *"I've been looking for a tool like this"* (Dex)
- *"I actually took a lot of inspiration from this resource for my last two AI agents videos. So, it's kind of awesome they agreed to be a sponsor"* (HubSpot)

**Селективность и влияние на продукт:**
- *"the only job search company I've agreed to a sponsorship with"* (Teal)
- *"I actually convinced teal to make changes to their Resume Builder to fit these standards"* (Teal)
- *"it's taught by actual Google instructors and not random influencers"* (Coursera)

**«Not sponsored but supported» — фирменная замена спонсорского слота своим продуктом** (стоит на том же месте, где был бы спонсор):
- *"this video is not sponsored but it is supported by those of you who subscribe to my paid productivity newsletter on Google tips Link in the description to learn more"* (многократно: Google's AI Course, You're using ChatGPT wrong, Prompt Formula, Gemini CORRECT way и др.)
- *"By the way, this video is not sponsored by Notion but it is supported by those of you who subscribe to my Paid Productivity newsletter and Community"* (Notion Daily Planner)
- *"by the way this video is not sponsored by Dr Watkins but it is supported by those of you who subscribe to my paid productivity newsletter"* (90-Day Plan from Harvard)
- *"this video is not being sponsored by Julie although that would be cool but it is supported by those of you who subscribe to my paid productivity newsletter"* (GREAT Manager)

**Анти-спонсорский гэг** (шутка о том, что бренды его НЕ спонсируют, — усиливает честность и одновременно подводит к своему продукту):
- *"By the way, neither Google nor Microsoft is sponsoring me for some reason, but this video is supported by those of you who subscribed to my Paid Productivity Newsletter"* (8 Productivity Tips)
- *"by the way both open AI and Microsoft refused to sponsor this video for whatever reason but it is supported by..."* (I wish every Job Seeker...)
- *"by the way Apple still refuses to sponsor me because I'm apparently too sarcastic whatever that means but this video is supported by..."* (New MacBook Pro)
- *"by the way neither Deloitte nor UI is sponsoring this video so KPMG PWC hit me up"* (Cover Letter Rejected)
- *"And no, Google is not sponsoring this video. Although, I really wish they would. Maybe I'm just not PC enough."* (Cinematic AI)
- *"it sounds like they're sponsoring me they're not"*, *"hashtag not sponsored"* (10 Best Mac Apps)
- *"first this amazing video is still not being sponsored by notion for some reason"* (How to Take Notes)

**Честность как фон доверия** (работает и на рекламу): в обзорах всегда есть блок недостатков (*"I say theoretically because I simply can't recommend the extension right now for three practical reasons"*, *"the examples they give in the course are pretty vague"*), советы не переплачивать (*"although I pay for perplexity Pro most users will be just fine with a free version"*, *"the vast majority of users do not need to upgrade"*), пометки *"None of which are sponsors, by the way."*

## 4. Лестница собственных продуктов

Собственная монетизация выстроена воронкой «бесплатно → регулярный контакт → платно». На каждом уровне свои дословные формулы.

### Ступень 1. Бесплатные шаблоны/промпты (лид-магниты)
«Валюта щедрости» — почти в каждом видео что-то отдаётся *"down below"*, часто это вынесено в заголовок: **«(template included)»** встречается в заголовках 6 раз, плюс «(Free Template)», «(with Examples)».
- *"because I love you all so much, I've actually included this Google Doc, down in the description"* (Gmail Tips)
- *"because I'm such an angel I've actually created a template in canva that you can use for free I'll link that down below"* (LinkedIn 8 Things)
- *"because I'm such an angel, I've prepared three starter templates for you"* (Claude Cowork System)
- *"By the way, I have a free AI toolkit that cuts through the noise and helps you master essential AI tools and workflows. I'll leave a link to that down below."* (повторяется дословно в AI-видео: AI Agents, Cinematic AI, 3 AI Habits, Claude, 95% Prompt Wrong...)
- *"you definitely want to grab my free workspace toolkit as well"* (Google's Prompting Guide)
- *"I'll leave a link to my free Cowork Toolkit below"* (Learn AI From Scratch)
- *"don't worry I've done most of the heavy lifting"* + *"I've added a link in the description to this partial template for you to duplicate"* (Prompts Database)
- Ранняя версия ступени — Facebook-группа: *"By the way, I have a Facebook group where I share weekly tips. Consider joining if you haven't already. I'll link it down below."*

### Ступень 2. Newsletter (регулярный контакт)
- *"you might want to join my newsletter to receive an insanely actionable Google workspace tip every week link down below"* — дословно повторяющийся блок во множестве видео (формула **"insanely actionable tip every week"**).
- *"By the way I do have a no-BS newsletter called the 'Productivity Ping.' I send you one practical tip and one keyboard shortcut every other week. So, sign up for that if you're interested. Link down below."* (MacBook Tips)
- Поздняя версия с квантификацией: *"if you want to boost your Google Workspace productivity by 1% every week... sign up for my weekly newsletter. Every issue is a bite-size tip you can read and apply in under 60 seconds."* (Core Workflow / NotebookLM)
- Платная рассылка/комьюнити продаётся через формулу "not sponsored but supported" (см. раздел 3) и бонусы: *"in addition to weekly productivity tips you get access to my AMA ask me anything sessions"* (Beginner to Pro).

### Ступень 3. Платный курс / waitlist
- Workspace Academy: *"you might want to check out my workspace Academy course where you'll learn a powerful workflow designed to eliminate mental and digital clutter link down below"* (Google Sheets, Gemini, Google Keep, Google Tasks...); в финале «The Productivity System I Taught to 6,642 Googlers»: *"I highly recommend checking out the full Workspace Academy course... I'll leave a link to the course down below."*
- Waitlist нового курса (прогрев до запуска): *"I'm actually developing an entire course on evergreen skills... If you're interested in learning a practical and timeless AI system, click the link below to join the weight list"* (дословно повторяется в нескольких AI-видео); *"I'm putting together a Co-worker Academy course, and you can sign up for the waitlist down below"* (Claude Cowork); *"I'm actually in the middle of creating entire course that teaches you how to create your own Command Center so I'll leave a link to the weit list down below"* (Prompts Database).
- Социальное доказательство при продаже: *"If you like to join over 5,500 students who are already using my exact system, check the link in the description"* (AI Agent Tutorial); *"click the link below to join 5,000 other students"* (ChatGPT 2026).
- Микропродукт: *"I put together something called Essential Power Prompts. It's a notion library of 15 battle tested prompts... Link down below"* (Gemini 3.1) с обоснованием: *"I genuinely believe you don't need a thousand random prompts. You need 10 to 15 battle tested ones... if you want to skip the trial and error."*

**Принцип размещения:** лид-магнит выдаётся в момент максимальной релевантности (сразу после соответствующего приёма), newsletter — в середине через "by the way", курс — в середине или финале через "speaking of systems...". Самоирония снимает неловкость продажи: *"I've actually put together a starter pack of instructions beginners can just copy and paste immediately out of the goodness of my heart, obviously, and not at all because I'm trying to build a parasocial relationship so that one day you'll buy my paid products. What? No, no, that's just obviously a joke."*

## 5. Награда за досмотр как CTA

Бонус в конце видео анонсируется заранее (open loop) и часто совмещён с лид-магнитом — досмотр конвертируется в клик по ссылке:

- *"as a reward for those of you who stayed until now I've included all the prompts from this video in a link down below"* (LinkedIn backed by data)
- *"as a reward for those of you who stayed until the end I've listed out all the prompts mentioned in this video on a single page"* (Land a Job using ChatGPT)
- *"To reward those of you who have stuck around until now, here's a link to download this exact template"* (Cover Letter: 3 Golden Rules)
- *"bonus tip for those of you who stuck around this far"* (Top 7 Gmail Tips)
- *"Bonus tip for everyone still watching, embedding AI triggers"* (3 AI Habits)
- *"As a bonus for those of you still watching, here are three of my favorite commands..."* (ChatGPT 2026)
- *"Here's a bonus update for those of you still watching."* (Gemini 3.1)
- *"Easter egg for those of you who didn't catch this at the beginning of this video."* (11 Extensions)
- Анонс-петля в начале: *"make sure to stick around to the very end because I have one small hack designed, especially for you"* (Google Calendar); *"make sure to stay to the very end for our cover letter formatting power tip that I promise you've never heard of before"* (Cover Letter).
- Фирменная фраза-оправдание бонуса: *"it wouldn't be a Jeffs video without a bonus tip"* (Get Started with Notion).

## 6. Перекрёстная воронка между видео

Каждый финал — не прощание, а маршрутизация. Ритуальная формула: краткий рекап → вопрос в комментарии → **конкретное** следующее видео или плейлист → *"See you on the next video. In the meantime, have a great one."* Механики:

1. **Финал = следующий шаг воркфлоу зрителя:** *"alright once you've updated that resume make sure to check out this video on how to write an effective cover letter to maximize your chances for a first round interview"* (Resume Mistakes → Cover Letter); *"now that you have an All-Star LinkedIn profile optimized for job search make sure to check out this video next on best practices for messaging Recruiters on LinkedIn"*.
2. **Последний совет специально завязан на другой туториал:** *"tip number 10 is knowing when to snooze... If you have no idea what I'm talking about, check out my step-by-step tutorial on inbox zero"* (Gmail Tips); *"always hyperlink the spreadsheet with the appropriate URL if you don't know what I'm talking about highly recommend you check out my video on how to take productive team notes"*.
3. **Кластеры-плейлисты:** AI-туториалы ссылаются друг на друга кольцом (*"check out my perplexity and notebook LM tutorials next"*, *"check out my claw tutorial next"*, *"you probably want to know when to use Google Gemini versus ChachiBT"*); карьерные — на плейлист интервью (*"make sure to check out my playlist on the most common interview questions and answers"*).
4. **Сериализация:** *"by the way this video is part one... next week i share the best mac apps i use for file and media management so make sure to be subscribed for those"*; *"in part two we'll go over how to use ChatGPT to network and land interviews in the first place... so make sure you're subscribed for that video"*; *"As a reminder, I'll cover the remaining two categories in part two, so keep an eye out for that."*
5. **Закладка плейлиста как привычки:** *"I'm going to make a lot more co-work tutorials, so I recommend you bookmark this co-work playlist."*
6. **Внутри видео — постоянные отсылки к своим прошлым роликам** вместо повторения материала: *"I have an entire video on how to set this up so I won't waste your time here"*, *"If you've watched my Google Calendar video, you know..."* — это и экономия времени, и session time.

## 7. Вовлечение в комментарии: типы вопросов-CTA

Комментарии у Джеффа — одновременно удержание, соцсигнал и фабрика идей. Основные типы с примерами:

1. **«Какой совет любимый?»** (лёгкий вопрос-выбор): *"please let me know down the comments which one of these tips you enjoyed the most"*; *"let me know down in the comments, which one is your favorite"*.
2. **Сбор заявок на будущие видео** (валидация тем): *"let me know in the comments what type of AI agent you'd like me to make a tutorial on next"*; *"let me know if you want a full tutorial on Sora 2"*; *"if you made it this far please comment if You' like me to create a structured job search toolkit you can download for free"*; *"let me know in the comments if you want an adjustable rate mortgage calculator"*; *"i can literally do an entire video on this app let me know if that's what you want"*.
3. **Краудсорсинг экспертизы** (зритель учит автора): *"what is a formula that you use a lot but is very uncommon?... Let me know down below"*; *"If you know other cool things uBlock Origin can do, let me know in the comments"*; *"any Hazel users, let me know if it's worth it and what you use it for"*; с самоиронией: *"please let me know down in the comment section below, because I love to learn from you guys and take credit for it in my next Notion video."*
4. **Личное применение зрителем:** *"let me know down in the comments below, which questions you will use in your next coffee chat"*; *"comment down below if you plan on using it for your next opportunity"*.
5. **Обещание ответной услуги автора** (комментарий = мини-консультация): *"let me know what weaknesses you plan on sharing in your next interview and I can give you some feedback in the comments below"*; *"commenting down below with a link to your LinkedIn profile so I can check it out"*; *"Let me know down in the comments section below what roles you're applying for, and maybe I can reply with some hook ideas"*; подкрепляется репутацией: *"many viewers are surprised I'm still replying to comments on videos I made 2 years ago."*
6. **Игровые/мемные CTA:** *"actually drop the calendar emoji down below if you're still watching so i know who's cool"*; *"drop a like if you didn't know that"*; *"feel free to hit that dislike button twice to really make your point"*; *"if you haven't found this useful at all, make sure to click that thumbs up button twice"*; лайк, вшитый в контент: *"common mistakes i've seen include... and third not giving this video a like if you found it helpful."*
7. **Интерактивный челлендж посреди видео:** *"now without watching the next part of this video let me know down in the comments how you might use this tool and we'll see if my examples cover your use case. all right no cheating"*; *"pause the video and see if you can spot the mistake"*; *"someone the comments please confirm this is indeed pronounced latex"*.
8. **Валидация нового формата/рубрики:** *"please do let me know whether you're enjoying this think outside the box episode i have way more like work hacks like this but i'm just not sure whether they're relevant for you"*.
9. **Mid-roll чекпоинт после первого пункта** (а не только в конце): *"Have you found this first tip helpful? Let me know by dropping a like and commenting down below which industry you wanna learn more about using this feature"*; *"If you found this first part helpful, drop a like and comment down below"*.
10. **Мнение/дискуссия:** *"I'd love to hear your thoughts on these trends, so drop them down below"*; *"the comment section will definitely have an opinion on the best text expander app, so why not click the like button on the way down there"*; *"let me know in the comments if this was news to you as well"*.

**Общий принцип:** вопрос всегда конкретен (не «что думаете?», а «какой из N советов», «какая формула», «какая тема следующая»), размещается сразу после релевантного пункта и часто обещает действие автора в ответ.
